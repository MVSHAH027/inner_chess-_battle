📖 Game Rules — How to Play

🎯 Objective — Conquer the 6 Inner Demons

The program places 6 Symbolic Soldiers and 1 Start Position (Golden Beacon) on the 3D chessboard. Conquer as many as possible in 6 moves!

💘 Lust: Rose Heart-Flame
💢 Anger: Scarlet Fury Spikes
💰 Greed: Gold Coin-Stack Gem
🎭 Deceit: Split Light/Dark Mask
👑 Ego: Tall Imperial Crown
🌀 Delusion: Orbiting Illusion Ring

♟ Your 6 Elements (Use Each Once, Any Sequence)

🐪 2 Camels (Bishops): Move diagonally any number of clear squares.
🐘 2 Elephants (Rooks): Move straight (horizontally/vertically) any number of clear squares.
🐴 2 Horses (Knights): Move in an L-shape (2+1) and can jump over pieces!

📈 Difficulty & Game Over

• Levels progress automatically after each game: Easy → Medium → Difficult.
• Game ends when all 6 Demons are conquered, all 6 moves are used, or no valid step remains.
• Scoring: +100 pts per Demon conquered, plus +200 bonus for conquering all 6!

✨ Trick ("Show Me") & 3D Controls

• Click Show Me anytime to watch a slow 3D animation revealing how all 6 can be conquered!
• 3D Camera: Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap/click a highlighted square (or Demon) to strike.
